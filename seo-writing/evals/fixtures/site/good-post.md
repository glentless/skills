Rinse the pan while it is still warm, scrub with coarse salt, dry it on the burner, then wipe on a thin film of oil. That is the whole routine, and it takes about three minutes.

> **Key Takeaways**
> - Warm water and salt clean almost everything.
> - Dry on heat, every time, or it rusts.

## Step 1: Rinse while warm

Food releases easily from a warm pan. Lodge, the manufacturer, recommends washing by hand with a small amount of soap ([Lodge care guide](https://www.lodgecastiron.com/cleaning-and-care)).

![A cast iron skillet being rinsed under a kitchen tap](/images/rinse.jpg)

See [how to re-season a rusty pan](/blog/reseason-cast-iron/) if yours is already damaged.

## Step 2: Dry on the burner

Water left on the surface is what causes rust. Two minutes over medium heat is enough.
